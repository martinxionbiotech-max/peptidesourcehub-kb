# Global Fulfillment & Logistics

## Fulfillment Center

PeptideSourceHub operates its primary fulfillment center in **Shenzhen, China**, strategically located within the Guangdong-Hong Kong-Macao Greater Bay Area. This location provides direct access to major international air cargo hubs (Hong Kong International Airport, Shenzhen Bao'an International Airport, Guangzhou Baiyun International Airport), enabling efficient global distribution.

## Temperature Monitoring & Cold Chain

| Feature | Availability |
|---|---|
| **Cold Chain Shipping (2–8°C)** | Available on request; uses validated insulated containers with phase-change coolant packs |
| **Ambient Shipping** | Standard for lyophilized peptides stable at ambient temperature for ≤14 days |
| **Temperature Data Loggers** | USB PDF loggers available as an add-on; provides time-stamped temperature history throughout transit |
| **Real-time Monitoring** | Available for high-value bulk shipments; GPS + temperature telemetry via cellular network |

Most lyophilized peptides are shipped at ambient temperature. Cold chain shipping is recommended for:

- International shipments >7 days transit
- Bulk orders with extended shelf-life requirements
- Temperature-sensitive formulations (reconstituted, liquid-stable formats)

## Free Shipping Threshold

Orders with a total invoice value exceeding **$800 USD** qualify for **complimentary express shipping** worldwide. This applies to the standard carrier selection for the destination region.

## Customs Documentation Package

Every international shipment includes a complete customs documentation package:

| Document | Description |
|---|---|
| **Commercial Invoice** | Detailed line-item invoice with HS codes, country of origin, declared value, and incoterms |
| **Packing List** | Item-level list with quantities, net/gross weights, and package dimensions |
| **Certificate of Analysis (COA)** | Batch-specific COA for each product in the shipment |
| **Material Safety Data Sheet (MSDS)** | Safety documentation for each peptide product |
| **Certificate of Origin** | Provided upon request; issued by China Council for the Promotion of International Trade (CCPIT) |
| **Import Declaration Assistance** | Pre-completed import declaration drafts for US FDA, EU customs, and AU TGA upon request |

## Packaging Standards

### Outer Packaging

- **Discreet:** Plain, unbranded corrugated cardboard boxes. No peptide or chemical identifiers on outer packaging.
- **Compliant Inner Labeling:** All required product identification, batch numbers, and regulatory statements are present on inner packaging in compliance with destination-country regulations.
- **Packaging Integrity:** Tamper-evident security seals on all inner containers.

### Inner Packaging

- Vacuum-sealed, multi-layer moisture-barrier pouches
- Desiccant packs included for moisture protection
- Amber glass vials with PTFE-lined caps
- Batch-traceable serial numbers on all primary containers

## Regional Shipping Notes

| Region | Note |
|---|---|
| **EU (EU MDR/IVDR)** | Shipments are classified as laboratory research materials. CE marking is not required for research-use-only peptides intended for in vitro investigation. |
| **Australia** | All shipments comply with TGA scheduling for laboratory research materials. Customs declarations include explicit "Research Use Only — Not for Human Use" notation. |
