## Research Background

ARA-290 is an 11-amino acid peptide engineered from the helix B domain of erythropoietin (EPO). The key innovation behind ARA-290 is its receptor selectivity: it activates the Innate Repair Receptor (IRR, also known as the β-common receptor/CD131 heterodimer) but does not bind the classical EPO receptor (EPO-R). This receptor discrimination separates tissue-protective signaling from erythropoietic signaling — in research models, ARA-290 demonstrates tissue protection and anti-inflammatory effects without elevating hematocrit.

### Key Mechanisms

| Mechanism | Details |
|---|---|
| **IRR Agonism** | Selective Innate Repair Receptor (CD131/β-common receptor) activation → no EPO-R binding |
| **JAK2/STAT3 Pathway** | Anti-inflammatory signaling cascade → suppression of pro-inflammatory cytokines |
| **Anti-Apoptotic** | Bcl-2 upregulation; caspase-3 inhibition; mitochondrial membrane stabilization |
| **No Erythropoiesis** | Engineered to avoid EPO-R homodimer → no hematocrit elevation or thrombosis risk |

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1: What is ARA-290 and how does it differ from EPO?

ARA-290 is an 11-amino acid peptide derived from the helix B domain of erythropoietin. It selectively activates the Innate Repair Receptor (IRR/CD131) without binding the classical EPO receptor — providing tissue protection without erythropoiesis (no increase in hematocrit). This receptor selectivity is the defining feature.

### Q2: What research applications is ARA-290 used for?

Primarily neuroprotection (small fiber neuropathy, peripheral nerve injury models), cardiac ischemia-reperfusion injury reduction, wound healing (chronic and diabetic ulcer models), renal protection, and neuroinflammation research.

### Q3: Why doesn't ARA-290 affect red blood cell production?

EPO's tissue-protective and erythropoietic activities are mediated by distinct receptor complexes. ARA-290 was specifically engineered to only activate the IRR/CD131 heterodimer (tissue protection) and not the EPO-R homodimer (erythropoiesis).

### Q4: How should ARA-290 be stored?

Lyophilized powder: 2–8°C, desiccated, 24-month stability. After reconstitution in sterile water or PBS, aliquot single-use portions and store at -20°C. Avoid freeze-thaw cycles.
