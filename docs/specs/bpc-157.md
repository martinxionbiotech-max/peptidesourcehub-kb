## Research Applications

BPC-157 is a synthetic peptide derived from a protective protein found in human gastric juice. The peptide is the subject of extensive preclinical investigation across multiple therapeutic areas:

### Primary Research Domains

| Area | Key Findings |
|---|---|
| **Tendon & Ligament Healing** | Accelerates fibroblast proliferation and collagen organization at injury sites; improves biomechanical recovery in animal tendon transection models |
| **Angiogenesis** | Promotes VEGF receptor expression and new capillary formation; facilitates granulation tissue development |
| **Gastrointestinal Protection** | Counteracts NSAID-induced lesions in gastric and intestinal mucosa; maintains endothelial integrity in experimental colitis models |
| **CNS Injury & Neuroprotection** | Attenuates traumatic brain injury outcomes; promotes neuronal survival and axonal regeneration in spinal cord injury models |
| **Bone Healing** | Enhances osteoblast activity and accelerates callus formation in fracture models |

### Mechanisms of Action

| Mechanism | Description |
|---|---|
| **VEGF Upregulation** | Increases vascular endothelial growth factor expression, driving angiogenesis and perfusion at injury sites |
| **Growth Hormone Receptor Modulation** | Modulates GH receptor expression in fibroblasts, enhancing proliferative response during repair phases |
| **Nitric Oxide (NO) System** | Preserves endothelial NO synthase activity; maintains vasodilatory and cytoprotective signaling |
| **FAK-Paxillin Pathway** | Activates focal adhesion kinase (FAK) and paxillin signaling, promoting fibroblast migration and focal adhesion turnover essential for wound closure |
| **Dopaminergic & Serotonergic Systems** | Modulates central neurotransmitter systems, contributing to observed neuroprotective effects |
