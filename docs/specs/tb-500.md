## Biological Context

Thymosin Beta-4 (Tβ4) is a 43-amino acid polypeptide (MW ~4,921 Da) present in most mammalian tissues. It was originally identified as a thymic hormone but is now recognized as the principal G-actin sequestering peptide in eukaryotic cells. The synthetic fragment TB-500 corresponds to the N-terminal actin-binding domain of Tβ4 (residues 17–23, LKKTETQ), which is essential for its actin-polymerization regulatory functions.

The fragment notation "17–23" refers to the amino acid positions within the full Tβ4 sequence, not molecular weight or any other parameter.

## Research Applications

| Research Domain | Description |
|---|---|
| **Cell Migration** | Promotes directional cell migration (chemotaxis) in fibroblasts, keratinocytes, and endothelial cells; key mediator of wound re-epithelialization |
| **Angiogenesis** | Stimulates endothelial cell differentiation into capillary-like structures; upregulates VEGF and matrix metalloproteinase expression |
| **Wound Healing** | Accelerates full-thickness dermal wound closure; increases collagen deposition and wound tensile strength |
| **Actin Polymerization** | Binds G-actin monomers (Kd ~0.7 μM) and regulates the G-actin/F-actin equilibrium; facilitates localized actin filament assembly at the leading edge of migrating cells |
| **Anti-inflammatory Activity** | Suppresses neutrophil infiltration and inflammatory cytokine release at injury sites |
| **Cardiac Protection** | Preclinical models of myocardial infarction show reduced scar size and improved ejection fraction |
| **Hair Follicle Cycling** | Promotes anagen phase induction and prolongs active growth phase in hair follicle models |

## Mechanisms of Action

| Mechanism | Description |
|---|---|
| **Actin Sequestration** | The LKKTETQ motif binds ATP-G-actin monomers with high affinity, maintaining a pool of polymerization-competent actin for rapid filament assembly |
| **Chemotactic Signaling** | TB-500 acts as a chemoattractant for endothelial cells, fibroblasts, and keratinocytes, guiding migration toward injury sites |
| **Pro-angiogenic Gene Expression** | Upregulates VEGF, bFGF, and MMP-2 expression through PI3K/Akt and NF-κB signaling cascades |
| **Anti-apoptotic Effects** | Reduces caspase-3 activation and increases Bcl-2/Bax ratio in cells subjected to oxidative stress |

## Special Notes

- **FOR LABORATORY RESEARCH USE ONLY.**
- TB-500 is a synthetic fragment and is not isolated from biological sources. The product does not contain thymic tissue extracts or any biologically derived raw materials.
- The G-actin binding affinity of the LKKTETQ fragment is retained at non-physiological pH (5.5–8.5 range), but experimental protocols should consider buffer conditions for optimal activity.
- As a short linear peptide, TB-500 is susceptible to non-specific adsorption to plastic labware. Use siliconized or low-retention vials and pipette tips for dilute solutions.
