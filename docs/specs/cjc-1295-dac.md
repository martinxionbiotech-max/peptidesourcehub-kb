## Research Background

CJC-1295 is a synthetic analogue of Growth Hormone-Releasing Hormone (GHRH) engineered with four amino acid substitutions that enhance receptor binding and proteolytic stability. The defining feature of the DAC (Drug Affinity Complex) variant is a maleimidopropionyl linker on the C-terminal lysine that covalently binds to Cys³⁴ of serum albumin — extending circulating half-life from ~7 minutes to ~8 days. For research protocols requiring chronic GH/IGF-1 axis activation, CJC-1295 with DAC is the tool of choice.

### Key Mechanisms

| Mechanism | Details |
|---|---|
| **GHRH-R Agonism** | Full GHRH receptor activation → cAMP → GH synthesis and release from pituitary |

| **IGF-1 Cascade** | Sustained GH → hepatic IGF-1 production → systemic anabolic signaling |

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1: What is the DAC in CJC-1295?

DAC stands for Drug Affinity Complex — a maleimidopropionyl linker that covalently binds to Cys³⁴ of serum albumin. This conjugation extends half-life from minutes to ~8 days by preventing renal clearance and enzymatic degradation.

### Q2: Difference between CJC-1295 with DAC and without DAC?

With DAC: ~8 day half-life → sustained GHRH activation → chronic GH/IGF-1. Without DAC: ~30 min half-life → discrete GH pulse → mimics endogenous pulsatile secretion. Choice depends on research objective.

### Q3: Why pair CJC-1295 with Ipamorelin?

CJC-1295 amplifies the GHRH signal (the 'on' switch). Ipamorelin suppresses somatostatin tone (the 'off' switch). Together they produce a larger net GH pulse than either alone — well-characterized synergism.

### Q4: How should CJC-1295 (DAC) be stored?

Lyophilized: 2–8°C, desiccated, 24-month stability. After reconstitution, store at 2–8°C. The DAC-albumin conjugate forms spontaneously — this is expected and part of the molecule's designed mechanism.

### Q5: What configurations are available?

Standard: 2mg × 10 vials. Bulk: 5mg × 10 vials. Custom mg/vial for OEM/private-label. CJC-1295 (no DAC) also available as a separate SKU for pulsatile GH protocols.

## Stability & Storage

| Condition | Degradation Profile |
|---|---|
| **Lyophilized Storage** | 24 months at 2–8°C in desiccated environment, protect from light |
| **DAC Linker Stability** | The maleimidopropionyl linker is reactive with free thiols; keep away from reducing agents (DTT, β-mercaptoethanol) which may cleave the DAC-albumin bond |
| **Neutral pH (6.0–7.5)** | Stable; recommended for reconstitution |
| **Albumin Conjugation** | The DAC linker covalently binds Cys³⁴ of serum albumin spontaneously in solution — this is the designed mechanism, not degradation |
| **Reconstituted Solution** | 24 h at 2–8°C. The DAC-albumin conjugate forms in solution — expect mass shift on analytical characterization. Avoid repeated freeze-thaw |
