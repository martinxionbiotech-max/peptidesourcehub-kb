## Research Background

AOD-9604 is the synthetic C-terminal fragment of human Growth Hormone, spanning amino acids 177 through 191. This 16-amino acid cyclic peptide retains the lipolytic domain of hGH while lacking the N-terminal region required for Growth Hormone Receptor (GHR) binding and subsequent IGF-1 induction. The result is a precision tool for studying adipose tissue lipolysis without the confounding effects of the GH/IGF-1 axis — including insulin resistance, IGF-1 elevation, and growth-promoting activity.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1: What is AOD-9604 and how does it relate to HGH?

AOD-9604 is the synthetic C-terminal fragment (amino acids 177-191) of human Growth Hormone. It retains the lipolytic domain of hGH without the GHR binding region — meaning it stimulates fat breakdown without raising IGF-1 levels.

### Q2: Does AOD-9604 affect blood glucose like full HGH?

No. Unlike full-length hGH (which can induce insulin resistance via its diabetogenic domain), the 177-191 fragment does not impair glucose tolerance. The diabetogenic domain of hGH resides outside the 177-191 sequence.

### Q3: What is the role of the disulfide bond in AOD-9604?

The Cys⁷–Cys¹⁴ disulfide bond is essential for the cyclic structure and biological activity. It constrains the peptide into the conformation required for β3-adrenergic receptor interaction. Verified by Ellman's test in every batch.

### Q4: What purity specification applies to AOD-9604?

≥99% by HPLC at 214 nm with ESI-MS identity confirmation and Ellman's test for disulfide verification. Batch-specific COA included.

### Q5: What is the difference between AOD-9604 and full-length HGH?

HGH (191 amino acids, ~22,125 Da) activates all GH receptor pathways including IGF-1 elevation and insulin resistance. AOD-9604 (16 amino acids, ~1,815 Da) is a precision fragment targeting only the lipolytic domain.
