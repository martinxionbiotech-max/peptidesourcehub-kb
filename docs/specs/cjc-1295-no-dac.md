## Research Background

CJC-1295 without DAC (also known as Mod GRF 1-29) is the same 30-amino acid GHRH analogue as the DAC variant, but without the C-terminal maleimidopropionyl linker. This single structural difference produces a fundamentally different pharmacokinetic profile: instead of 8-day sustained GH elevation, the no-DAC variant has a short ~30-minute half-life producing a discrete GH pulse that closely mimics endogenous pulsatile secretion patterns.

### Key Mechanisms

| Mechanism | Details |
|---|---|
| **GHRH-R Agonism** | Full receptor activation → cAMP → GH synthesis and release from somatotrophs |
| **Short Half-Life** | ~30 minute plasma half-life → discrete GH pulse → mimics endogenous GHRH |
| **No Albumin Binding** | Absence of DAC linker → no serum albumin conjugation → renal clearance at normal rate |

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1: Why choose CJC-1295 without DAC?

Choice depends on research objective. No DAC = ~30 min half-life = discrete physiological GH pulse. With DAC = ~8 day half-life = sustained GH/IGF-1 elevation. Use no DAC for pulsatile GH studies; use DAC for chronic activation.

### Q2: Is CJC-1295 (no DAC) the same as Mod GRF 1-29?

Essentially yes. Both refer to the same 30-amino acid GHRH analogue with four substitutions but without the DAC linker. Both terms describe the short-acting, non-albumin-binding variant.

### Q3: What is the optimal partner peptide?

Ipamorelin is preferred for pulsatile protocols: CJC-1295 provides GHRH signal, Ipamorelin suppresses somatostatin tone → synergistic, clean GH pulse. GHRP-2 can be used when maximum pulse amplitude is preferred over selectivity.

### Q4: What is the half-life difference vs DAC variant?

No DAC: ~30 minutes → discrete GH pulse. With DAC: ~8 days (via albumin conjugation). This ~400-fold difference is entirely due to the maleimidopropionyl DAC linker.

### Q5: How is absence of DAC verified?

HPLC-MS confirms the molecular ion without the maleimidopropionyl mass adduct (+97 Da absent vs DAC variant). Included in every batch-specific COA.
