## Research Background

Thymosin Beta-4 is a naturally occurring 43-amino acid polypeptide found in virtually all mammalian cells. It was originally identified as the thymic hormone responsible for T-cell maturation, but subsequent research revealed its primary intracellular function: G-actin sequestration. By maintaining the cellular actin monomer pool, Thymosin Beta-4 regulates cytoskeletal dynamics critical for cell migration, wound healing, and angiogenesis. Unlike its synthetic fragment TB-500, full-length Thymosin Beta-4 retains the complete N-terminal actin-binding domain and C-terminal regulatory regions, offering the full biological activity of the native protein.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1: What is the difference between TB-500 and Thymosin Beta-4?

TB-500 is the synthetic N-terminal fragment (Ac-LKKTETQ, ~889 Da) of the full 43-amino acid Thymosin Beta-4 protein. TB-500 retains the core actin-binding motif but lacks the full C-terminal regulatory sequence. Thymosin Beta-4 is the complete native protein with broader biological activity including additional binding domains and post-translational interaction sites.

### Q2: What research applications is Thymosin Beta-4 used for?

Primarily wound healing, angiogenesis, cardiac protection, and tissue regeneration research. Its actin-sequestering activity makes it a key tool in cytoskeletal dynamics studies. Also investigated in dermal repair, corneal healing, and hair follicle biology.

### Q3: How should Thymosin Beta-4 be stored and reconstituted?

Reconstitute with sterile water or PBS (pH 7.0–7.4). The full-length protein is slightly more complex to handle than TB-500 due to its larger size. Aliquot single-use portions and store at -20°C. Avoid freeze-thaw cycles. Lyophilized powder: 24-month stability at 2–8°C.

### Q4: Is Thymosin Beta-4 the same as Thymosin α1?

No. Thymosin α1 (28 amino acids, ~3,108 Da) and Thymosin β4 (43 amino acids, ~4,964 Da) are distinct proteins with different biological roles. Thymosin α1 is primarily immunomodulatory, while Thymosin β4 is primarily involved in actin dynamics and tissue repair.
