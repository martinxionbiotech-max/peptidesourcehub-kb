## Research Background

Semax is a synthetic heptapeptide developed at the Institute of Molecular Genetics of the Russian Academy of Sciences. It consists of the ACTH(4–7) fragment (Met-Glu-His-Phe) — the active core of adrenocorticotropic hormone that mediates neurotrophic effects without steroidogenic activity — extended with a C-terminal Pro-Gly-Pro tripeptide. Semax is the most extensively characterized nootropic peptide in the literature, with documented effects on BDNF and NGF expression, cognitive function, and neuroprotection across multiple paradigms.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1: What is Semax and how does it relate to ACTH?

Semax is a synthetic heptapeptide based on ACTH(4–7), the N-terminal fragment of ACTH that mediates neurotrophic effects without adrenal steroidogenesis. The C-terminal Pro-Gly-Pro extension confers metabolic stability and CNS penetration.

### Q2: What is the mechanism of Semax's cognitive effects?

Semax primarily upregulates BDNF and NGF via melanocortin receptor activation. Elevated neurotrophins support synaptic plasticity, neuronal survival, and cognitive function. Additional effects include anti-inflammatory microglial modulation and improved cerebral blood flow.

### Q3: How does the Pro-Gly-Pro extension work?

The C-terminal PGP tripeptide protects from carboxypeptidase degradation and enhances blood-brain barrier penetration. The Pro-Gly-Pro sequence itself has reported glyproline activity that may contribute to neuroprotection.

### Q4: How does Semax differ from Selank?

Both are Russian-developed nootropics with distinct mechanisms. Semax (ACTH-derived) → melanocortin receptors → BDNF/NGF → cognition. Selank (tuftsin-derived) → GABAergic/dopaminergic → anxiolysis. Complementary: Semax for cognition, Selank for anxiety.

### Q5: What purity and QC is provided for Semax?

≥99% HPLC with ESI-MS identity. Special QC: Met(O) content ≤ 1% (N-terminal methionine is oxidation-sensitive). Batch-specific COA included.
